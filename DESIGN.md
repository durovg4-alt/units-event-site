# UNITS EVENT — design direction

## Concept
“Light through a drink”: a dark, cinematic event world cut by warm translucent color. The page should feel like a controlled bar performance — precise, tactile and adult — rather than a generic luxury template.

## Tokens
- Oxblood `#3f0712`: primary brand field.
- Black cherry `#190106`: deep background and contrast.
- Champagne `#d9bd7c`: warmth, liquid highlights and focus.
- Porcelain `#f2ede6`: primary text and light surfaces.
- Smoke `#a7a3a0`: secondary copy.
- Sage `#7f8f78`: botanical accent used sparingly.

## Type
- Melody: all page headings and offer names; used as the brand's expressive display face.
- Transforma Mix: interface, body, navigation and price copy.
- Transforma Script: one expressive handwritten line in the closing contact section only.

## Layout
- Cinematic full-viewport hero with copy anchored left and documentary action right.
- Information moves from editorial intro to four equal-height photographic service cards, a full-bleed carousel, cocktail narrative and contact climax.
- Service cards use blurred event photography under a wine gradient; sticky positioning lets each incoming format overlap the previous one while scrolling.
- Left alignment dominates. Center alignment is reserved for the final contact scene.
- The header and hero share the same shell: the left edge resolves to the logo and the right edge to the primary contact action.
- The cocktail narrative uses one rounded square media stage with four named, switchable drinks.

## Motion
- One orchestrated hero entrance.
- Scroll-triggered masked type and grouped reveals.
- The service stack subtly scales the previous card as the next one arrives.
- Subtle image depth and magnetic CTAs on fine pointers only.
- Reduced-motion mode removes smooth scrolling, parallax and autoplay.

## Accessibility
- WCAG AA contrast, visible champagne focus ring, native links/buttons, minimum 44px touch targets.
- Carousel has pause and next controls with meaningful labels.
- Content remains visible without JavaScript and motion is non-essential.
